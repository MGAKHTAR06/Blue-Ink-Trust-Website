About Blue Ink Trust

Eradicating deprivation, marginalisation and exploitation — empowering the community through education and training.

Eradicating deprivation, marginalisation, exploitation — and empowering the community through education and training.

Who we are

Community-centred, education-led

We are steadfast in our conviction that every child, irrespective of background or circumstance, deserves the opportunity to thrive. Blue Ink Trust is a community-centred charitable organisation dedicated to advancing equitable access to education and cultivating meaningful opportunities for children and young people facing systemic disadvantages.

Our belief

Every child, irrespective of background or circumstance, deserves the opportunity to thrive — and no child should be shut out of a classroom because their family is poor.

Our work in the UK

In Birmingham, Blue Ink Trust delivers weekly supplementary education programmes designed to enhance core academic competencies, foster self-confidence, and address learning disparities. Our pedagogical strategies are carefully structured to empower children and young people to recognise, develop, and realise their full potential.

International work

Internationally, our initiatives strengthen the long-term prospects of children and young people in underserved rural communities. We provide sustained educational support from early childhood through to secondary education and progression into higher education, addressing the complex needs of orphaned, vulnerable, and socio-economically disadvantaged children and young people.

Our approach

Across all our projects, our work is underpinned by a commitment to long-term, sustainable impact. We aim to equip children and young people with the skills, confidence, and resilience required to overcome structural barriers and participate fully across all dimensions of social, educational, and civic life.
